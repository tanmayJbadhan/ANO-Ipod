Jigglypuff · assembly reference

PCM5122 on the left, SD card on the right

Every wire stays on its own header — nothing crosses the board. The PCM5122 sits on the left because that's the only side with an I2C bus; the SD card sits on the right because that's the only side with a true 3.3V pin.

Soldered and verified

Both headers are wired as shown below, with jumper colours recorded. Each header has 13 pins — BAT on the right (between 3V3 and GPIO38) and the second VBUS on the left are both correctly left empty, so no wire sits on the raw battery terminal.

Before you solder

  • SDA/SCL (GPIO7 / GPIO6) is the board's only I2C bus — the PCM5122's control line shares it with the joystick on the left STEMMA QT port. Different I2C addresses, so no conflict.
  • The SD card is 3V-only with no onboard regulator — don't substitute VBUS (5V) for its power pin, it needs the real 3V3 pin.
  • Leave the SD card's DET pin (card detect) unconnected — not used by the firmware.
  • The left header's two VBUS pads are the same net — wire only one.
  • Firmware pin map lives in 3_Music_App/SdConfig.h and 3_Music_App/AudioConfig.h; it must match whatever is physically soldered.
